Open Skills Institute

Frequently asked questions

What to know about access, certification, and our beta program.

Is OSI free?

Yes. Participation and digital certificate downloads are free during beta testing. You do not need to donate to take part.

Can anyone join right now?

OSI is currently in beta testing and access is by invitation only. If you have been invited, use the username and password provided by the organizer. Wider access and launch dates have not been announced.

Can I donate to support OSI?

Yes. Voluntary donations can help support ongoing development and improvements. Ask the organizer about available ways to contribute. Donations are optional and do not affect your access or certificate.

Can I use my certificate for employment?

You can include it in your professional development records or share it with an employer to describe the training topic. Each employer decides whether to accept it and whether it meets their requirements. It does not guarantee employment, professional licensing, or recognition.

Who creates the programs?

OSI programs are created by experts, with a focus on practical knowledge, clear learning outcomes, and skills relevant to professional communication and everyday work.

Will I receive a digital certificate?

Yes. The certificate tool creates a personalized, one-page landscape PDF with selectable text. It includes your name, the program title and description, training dates, 15 training hours, and a name-based reference code.

Which training is available?

Public Information Management and Effective Communication is currently available through the login and certificate flow. The other ten topics are marked Coming Soon. Browse the Trainings page to read all eleven descriptions.

How does online self-paced training work?

The study plan allocates 15 hours across five consecutive dates, with three hours per day and no fixed meeting times. Choose your start date; the end date is calculated four calendar days later. Learning activities and any completion requirements are arranged separately with the organizer.

What does the available program cover?

The program focuses on audience analysis, accurate and ethical public information, clear and accessible communication materials, selecting suitable channels, and using active listening and feedback to improve professional communication. These outcomes also appear on the certificate.

How do I download my certificate?

Open the available training and sign in with your assigned credentials. Enter your full name and start date, review the live certificate preview, then select Download certificate PDF directly below it.

Where do I find my certificate reference code?

Your reference code appears on the certificate and in the certificate details form. It is generated automatically from the participant name and is included in the downloaded PDF. Keep it with your professional development records.
